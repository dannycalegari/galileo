# Animals: what they look like, how they go about, and where they live (src/animals.cc).
#   animal ID                  a kind of animal, then its lines:
#     name a cow | cows        what it is called (one | many)
#     look TEXT                what L says of one
#     body four|bird LENGTH HEIGHT   the body's length and the height at the shoulder, in tiles
#                              (a bird: its height without the neck)
#     colour #hex ...          one of these, animal by animal
#     patches #hex             patches on the flank; head #hex: the head's own colour
#     horns wool mane snout ears neck comb tailup    what else it has
#     speed N                  tiles a second, walking
#     flock                    keeps close to the rest of its herd
#     stands                   does not lie down at night (horses sleep standing)
#     roams                    goes about the streets day and night, with short stops (dogs, cats)
#     use VERB [needs ITEM] [gives ITEM] [every MINUTES] | TEXT
#                              what U and a click on one does: the first use whose ITEM you carry
#                              (items.txt); it gives you ITEM, at most once in MINUTES; from
#                              somebody's animal (a herd's, a farm's, an inn's) only if nobody sees
#     done VERB TEXT           what you read if you try it again too soon
# Where they live (a town's animals are put out when you first come within reach of it):
#   pasture ID LO HI [in PLACES]   a herd of LO..HI on the grass outside every town (not hamlets)
#   yard ID LO HI [in PLACES]      in the yards and gardens of a farm or a village
#   inn ID LO HI                   tied up beside the street door of every inn
#   at "NAME" ID LO HI             round the door of a building whose name contains NAME
#   pen "NAME" ID LO HI            inside an open building whose name contains NAME (a market hall)
#   street ID LO HI [in PLACES]    loose in the streets and squares of every town
#   PLACES: towns or countries (the people's files' "country"; France where none is given),
#   with commas between and _ for a space

animal cow
  name a cow | cows
  look A cow, chewing, who looks at you without interest and goes on chewing.
  body four 1.6 1.12
  colour #6B4A2E #8A5A34 #3A2C24 #B89A78
  patches #EAE3D6
  horns
  speed 0.35
  use milk needs pail gives milk every 720 | You milk her into the pail, your forehead against her warm flank. She shifts her feet, and lets you.
  done milk She has been milked already today, and does not mean to be milked again.
  use scratch | You scratch her between the horns. She lets you. With a pail, you could milk her.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the wild ones (main.cc, Game::wildStep)
# Never put out by the rules above: met in the open country, away from the towns, and gone again
# once you are far from them. Their own lines:
#     wild                  one of these
#     hostile N             goes for you once you are within N tiles
#     shy N                 runs from you within N tiles (and from a shot)
#     run N                 tiles a second, at a run
#     fight HP SKILL DAMAGE it can be fought (C and a click) and shot (the fowling piece), and killed
#     flees F               runs off, wounded, below F of its health
#     night                 abroad at night (and at dusk and dawn in winter); lies up by day
#     antlers               a stag's (half of a herd are hinds, without)
#     every S               seconds between its bites or blows (1.4 if not given)
#     biters N              no more than N of its herd at you at once (a pack: the rest circle)
#     bout S                it breaks off after S seconds at you (a bear's charge)
#     (when one of a pack is killed, the rest run)
#     use VERB dead ...     what can be done with one that has been killed (skinned, butchered)
