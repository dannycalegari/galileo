CAP. XIV. //
Conclusio demonstrationis de sanguinis circuitu. //

Iam tandem nostram de circuitu sanguinis sententiam dicere, & omnibus proponere liceat. [...] necessarium est concludere, circulari quodam motu in circuitu agitari in animalibus sanguinem, & esse in perpetuo motu, & hanc esse actionem sive functionem cordis, quam pulsu peragit, & omnino motus & pulsus cordis causam unam esse.
=
CHAPTER XIV. //
The conclusion of the demonstration of the circuit of the blood. //

Now at last let me state our opinion of the circuit of the blood, and put it before everyone. [...] it is necessary to conclude that in animals the blood is driven round in a circuit, with a kind of circular motion, and is in perpetual motion; that this is the action, or function, of the heart, which it performs by its beat; and that this is the one and only cause of the motion and beat of the heart.
