QVÆSTIO VIII. //

Propositum quadratum diuidere in duos quadratos. //

[...]

In margine, manu Domini de Fermat: //
Cubum autem in duos cubos, aut quadratoquadratum in duos quadratoquadratos, & generaliter nullam in infinitum vltra quadratum potestatem in duos eiusdem nominis fas est diuidere: cuius rei demonstrationem mirabilem sane detexi. Hanc marginis exiguitas non caperet.
=
QUESTION VIII. //

To divide a given square into two squares. //

[...]

In the margin, in Monsieur de Fermat's hand: //
But it is impossible to divide a cube into two cubes, or a fourth power into two fourth powers, or in general any power beyond the square into two powers of the same kind; of which I have found a truly marvellous proof. This margin is too narrow to hold it.
