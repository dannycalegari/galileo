Most Beloved Lord Father, //

They tell me the Father Inquisitor has been to the house, and that you are ordered to Rome, to present yourself to the Holy Office within the month. I did not sleep last night, and I think you did not either, for I saw your light from our dormitory window until the bell rang for Matins.

Do not let them hurry you. You are sixty-eight, the roads are full of plague and quarantines, and Dottor Ronconi says the cold would do you more harm than any Cardinal. Let your friends act for you: the Grand Duke, and the stranger who carried your book away this summer, whom God sent us, I think, on purpose.

I am sending a little quince cheese by Geppo. Eat it, and do not give it all to La Piera. //

Your most affectionate daughter, //
Suor Maria Celeste //
From San Matteo in Arcetri, the first day of October 1632
