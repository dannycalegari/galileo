Most Beloved Lord Father, //

You left this morning before it was light, in the Grand Duke's litter, with snow on the hills, and I watched the lantern go down the lane until I could not see it any more. Rome has had its way: come, or be brought in chains. Twenty days of quarantine at the border, at Ponte a Centina, in a house where they say there is nothing to eat but eggs; I have sent eggs anyway, being a fool.

All Florence is talking of the King of Sweden, who was killed in a battle in Germany in November, in a fog, at a place called Lützen. They say his little daughter is Queen now. God have mercy on him, heretic or not, and on her.

I shall keep your house as if you were in it, and Signor Geri has promised to look in. //

Your most affectionate daughter, //
Suor Maria Celeste //
San Matteo, the twentieth of January 1633
