Most Beloved Lord Father, //

Signor Geri tells me you have come safe to Rome, and are lodged with the Ambassador at the Villa Medici, on the hill by the Trinità dei Monti, and that the Signora Ambassadress is as kind to you as a daughter. I am jealous of her, and grateful.

Here everything is as you left it. The lemons are wrapped against the frost, the wine is in the cellar, and your mule is eating its head off. Sister Arcangela sends her duty; she does not send much else, as you know.

Rome is a city of a thousand tongues, and some of them, they say, are not kind to you. Trust the Ambassador, and the friends God sends you there. I pray for you at every Office, and the sisters with me. Write to me, even a line, so that I can see your hand is steady. //

Your most affectionate daughter, //
Suor Maria Celeste //
San Matteo, the thirteenth of February 1633
