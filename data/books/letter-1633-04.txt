Most Illustrious Lord, //

Signor Galileo was called to the Holy Office this morning, the twelfth, and examined before the Commissary, Father Maculano. They have shown him a paper of the year sixteen, which says that he was commanded by Cardinal Bellarmine not to hold, teach or defend the opinion of Copernicus in any way whatever. He says he never received any such command, only Bellarmine's word that the opinion might not be held or defended, and that he has the Cardinal's own certificate to prove it. He did not bring it with him: he says it is in safe hands, and that he will want it again for his defence.

They have kept him there, in the rooms of the Fiscal, which are good rooms, with his own servant; it is a kindness, and it is a prison. I am told there is another paper spoken of in certain houses, which would settle everything against him. I do not know what it is. I am doing what I can. //

Your Lordship's most devoted servant, //
Francesco Niccolini //
Rome, the twelfth of April 1633
