Most Beloved Lord Father, //

Signor Geri tells me they will not let you come home: not to Siena, not to Arcetri, but to stay in Rome under guard, at His Holiness's pleasure. I read it twice to be sure, and then I went to the chapel and could not pray at all.

They gave you a penance, the seven penitential psalms once a week. I have taken it on myself, by leave of our Mother Abbess, so you need not trouble about it.

Our window looks south, towards Rome, though Rome is a long way off. I shall stand at it every evening after Compline. //

Your most affectionate daughter, //
Suor Maria Celeste //
San Matteo, the tenth of December 1633
