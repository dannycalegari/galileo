Most Beloved Lord Father, //

They say you will be home before Christmas: confined to the house, and forbidden to receive company, but home, and a quarter of a mile from me, which is nearer than Siena and nearer than Rome. The Archbishop of Siena has been kinder to you than you deserve, and I shall pray for him as long as I live.

I have something to confess. They gave you a penance, the seven penitential psalms once a week for three years. I have taken it on myself, by leave of our Mother Abbess, and I find them very pleasant to say, so you need not trouble about them at all.

The lemons are waiting, and the wine, and the mule, and I. //

Your most affectionate daughter, //
Suor Maria Celeste //
San Matteo, the tenth of December 1633
