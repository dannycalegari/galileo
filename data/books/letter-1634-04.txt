My dear Signor Geri, //

My daughter, Suor Maria Celeste, died on the second of this month, at thirty-three years old, after six days of a dysentery, and I find that I cannot write the words without stopping.

She was a woman of exquisite mind, singular goodness, and the most tender love for me. I am left in an immense sadness and melancholy; I do not eat, and I do not sleep, and at night I hear myself continually called by her, by name, as she used to call me from the gate.

They tell me the Holy Office was asked whether I might go to the funeral. I did not wait to hear the answer. //

Your most affectionate friend and servant, //
Galileo Galilei //
Arcetri, April 1634
