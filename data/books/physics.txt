LIBER SEPTIMVS. //
Caput I. //

Omne quod mouetur, necesse est ab aliquo moueri. Si enim in seipso non habet principium motus, manifestum est quod ab altero mouetur: aliud enim erit mouens.

In margine, manu studiosi: Et sagitta, postquam ab arcu recessit? Quid tum eam mouet? Roga Magistrum Lebrun. //
Et infra, alia manu: Aer, stulte.

BOOK SEVEN. //
Chapter I. //

Everything that is in motion must be moved by something. For if it does not have the source of its motion in itself, it is plain that it is moved by another: for the mover will be something else.

In the margin, in a student's hand: And the arrow, once it has left the bow? What moves it then? Ask Master Lebrun. //
And below, in another hand: The air, you fool.
