# Things: what lies about, what you can carry, and works of art.
#
#   item ID
#     name SINGULAR | PLURAL     "a loaf of bread | loaves of bread"
#     also WORD WORD ...         other words a player may call it by ("give paper": the document of 1616)
#     shape KIND #colour [#colour2]
#                                loaf jug cup plate candle book knife apples cabbage
#                                cheese fish horseshoe nails hammer tongs coil purse bottle
#                                firewood pot inkpot quill onions; for works of art:
#                                painting tomb canopy statue reliquary window none; larger than life:
#                                giant group pieta; baldacchino; urn fountain shrine lamp lectern
#     weight POUNDS
#     price DENIERS              12 deniers to the sou, 20 sous to the livre
#     money DENIERS              taking it gives you this much money (a purse)
#     fixed                      cannot be taken
#     chained                    fixed, and with a "book" line read where it lies by looking at it (L)
#                                (the chained books of the Bodleian)
#     title TEXT                 works of art: the heading of what you read
#     image FILE                 works of art: a picture shown with it (in data/, see art/CREDITS.txt)
#     credit TEXT                where the picture comes from, shown under it (required by its licence)
#     look TEXT                  what you see when you look at it
#     use eat|drink TEXT {EFFECTS}   using it (U): one is gone, and you read TEXT
#     heals N                    eating it mends N health points when you are hurt (a player has 20, plus
#                                4 for each point of fencing; a point mends by itself in twenty minutes);
#                                the text then ends "(It does you good.)"
#     use read TITLE | TEXT      a page to read     use say TEXT   just the TEXT
#     book ID                    it opens as a book you leaf through (data/books/ID.txt: pages in
#                                its own language, in English once you can read that language)
#     use make RESULT [N] | TEXT this (and what goes "with" it) becomes RESULT; with ITEM N; takes MINUTES
#     use play PIECE | TEXT      you play a piece of data/music.txt yourself
#     needs SKILL TEXT           without the skill (learnt from people: {player learn SKILL}) you read TEXT
#     night TEXT {EFFECTS}       what happens instead if you use it at night
#     near ITEM TEXT             it can be used only within two tiles of such a thing (else TEXT)
#     image, credit              (also for things you carry: shown when you read them)
#                                (effects as in roles.txt, on what you know: set KEY, add KEY N)
#   end
#
# Where things lie (each line is tried on every piece of furniture of that kind):
#   on FEATURE [in KIND | in "NAME"]: ITEM CHANCE ITEM CHANCE ...
#   beside FEATURE [in KIND | in "NAME"]: ITEM CHANCE ...     on the floor next to it
#       FEATURE: table counter stall chest bench altar barrel anvil oven hearth bed
#       KIND: the kind of building (house inn college church palace ...); "NAME": part
#       of a building's name. A CHANCE of 1 or less is a chance; more is a number of them.
#   art "NAME" wall: ITEM ITEM ...     works of art hung on the walls inside a building
#   art "NAME" floor: ITEM ...         standing on the floor inside it, spread out
#   art "NAME" row: ITEM ...           standing in a row, two tiles apart
#   art "NAME" upstairs: ITEM ...      along the outer walls of its upper floors, evenly spaced, clear of the
#                                      stairs (a college with library=NAME in its map legend)
#   art "NAME" court: ITEM ...         on the open floor of its courtyard; "court wall:" against the courtyard's walls
#   art "NAME" on FEATURE: ITEM ...    on a piece of furniture (an altar)
#   art statue in "TOWN": ITEM         at the statue of that town
#   art statue near "NAME": ITEM ...   each on the free statue nearest that building: shapes giant,
#                                      group and pieta on a pedestal ('y'), shape none as the rider ('s')
#   art "NAME" over FEATURE: ITEM      standing on the floor over it (the baldacchino over an altar)
#   art "NAME" behind altar: ITEM     high in the wall behind the altar (shape window: an east window)
#   art "NAME" left of altar: ITEM   beside the altar, on its left as you face it, against the side wall
#   moored [in "TOWN"]: ITEM          a boat at each mooring post of the towns ('g' in the maps)
