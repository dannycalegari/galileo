# The market: what each town makes (bought cheap there) and wants (sold dear there), as factors
# on a thing's price (items.txt "trade goods"); its customs, a part of the value of the goods you
# carry in, taken at the town's edge; its language (a skill: without it, a foreigner pays a tenth
# more and gets a tenth less).
#
#   market TOWN
#     makes ITEM FACTOR ITEM FACTOR ...
#     wants ITEM FACTOR ...
#     customs RATE
#     language SKILL
#   end
#
# The merchants of a market town (role merchant) sell what it makes; they buy what it wants
# (and shopkeepers buy back what they sell, at half price, as before). A town that has bought a
# lot of something lately pays less for more of it (People::glut: each one sold counts, and the
# count wears off by one a day). Coins and money-changers: data/coins.txt.
#
#     forbids ITEM ITEM ... | WHY     goods that may not come in at all: the searchers at the town's
#                                     edge find them (most times by day, a third of the time at night,
#                                     less often in a pack with a false bottom, never in one at night),
#                                     seize them and fine you their worth (a night in prison if you
#                                     cannot pay). They pay no customs, since nobody declares them. By
#                                     day the searchers are seen: you step back unsearched, unless you
#                                     come on again within the hour (Game::searchers).
