    amsterdam,holland: In this great city everyone is so busy with his own profit that I could live here all my life without being noticed by a soul. That is why I came.

person rembrandt
  name Rembrandt Harmensz van Rijn
  title a painter
  role painter
  look beard moustache
  near Uylenburgh
  work landmark Uylenburgh
  talk
    give:mother-letter: From my mother! (He reads it, and laughs, and wipes his eyes on his sleeve.) She says I am too proud, I do not eat, and I should marry a good girl from Leiden. Two of these are true. {take} {player set gave-mother-letter}
    mother,leiden,neeltgen if not player gave-mother-letter: My mother is in Leiden, by the Weddesteeg. I painted her reading her Bible. She writes to me every week, and I do not answer. Do not tell her.
    give:ultramarine: Ultramarine! (He opens the pouch and is silent a long while, then laughs.) Real lapis, from Venice. I shall paint a Virgin's robe with it, and a cardinal will buy it and never know who brought it. Here: take this. It is worth nothing now, but keep it. {take} {player add has:rembrandt-sketch 1} {player set gave-rembrandt}
    blue,ultramarine,colour,colours,paint if not player gave-rembrandt: Blue! I have no blue worth the name: smalt, that turns grey, and azurite, that turns green. Real ultramarine comes from Venice, from the colour-sellers under the Rialto, and costs more than gold. If you were ever there with money in your pocket...
    drawing,sketch if player gave-rembrandt: The old man? A beggar who sits by the Oude Kerk. I draw him when I cannot sleep.
    name: Rembrandt, Harmen's son, of Leiden. The miller's son. {name}
    job: I paint, here in Uylenburgh's house: portraits, mostly. They pay. This winter I painted the surgeons, Dr Tulp's anatomy lesson; now every merchant in Amsterdam wants to be painted by me.
    tulp,anatomy,surgeons: The doctor holds up the muscles of the forearm with his forceps, and shows how they move the fingers. Seven surgeons look on, and each of them paid to be in it, so each of them had to be seen.
    light,painting: Darkness, and light falling on what matters. The rest you leave to the eye.
    uylenburgh: Hendrick sells pictures, and I paint them. He has a cousin in Friesland, Saskia; he says I would like her.
  end
end

person tulp
  name Nicolaes Tulp
  title a physician
  role physician
  look beard tuft
  near Waag
  work landmark Waag
  talk
    name: Dr Nicolaes Tulp, praelector of anatomy to the Surgeons' Guild of Amsterdam. {name}
    job: I physic the living, and in winter I open the dead, before the guild and anyone who pays a ticket. The guild meets upstairs here in the Waag.
    anatomy,lesson,body: In January we had Aris Kindt, a thief, hanged that morning. Young Rembrandt painted it. I explained the muscles of the arm.
    tulp,tulip,name: My father was Pieterszoon; I took the name Tulp from the tulip on my house. Now they say tulips may cost as much as a house.
    tea: I recommend tea, from China. It purifies the blood. One day everyone in Holland will drink it.
  end
end

    old,map,squares,chequerboard if player read-old-map: That old map? Europe all in little squares, by somebody who wanted to make it simple. Holland is three islands in it! Some joker has written that the land came up out of the sea in a single night. Well: in a way we made it ourselves. Half of Holland was sea once, and we pumped it out with windmills.
    tube,tubes: Pasteboard tubes, covered in leather, for maps: two sous. A spectacle-maker bought three last week, I don't know why.
    name: Willem Jansz Blaeu, at the Sign of the Gilded Sundial, on the Water. {name}
    job: I make maps and globes and print books: atlases, sea-pilots, astronomy. My Light of Navigation is in every ship's cabin, and I hope the East India Company will soon want me for its charts.
    tycho,brahe: I studied with Tycho Brahe, on his island of Hven, when I was young. I helped him with his star catalogue. He had a nose of gold, and a tame elk.
    galileo,dialogue if stock:dialogue >= 1: Galileo's Dialogue? Two copies came up the Rhine with the Frankfurt books. They will not last.
    galileo,dialogue if heard-dialogue-in-town: Sold, both copies, one to that Frenchman who never gets up before noon. If Galileo ever wants something printed that they will not print in Italy, he should send it to Holland.
    galileo,dialogue: Galileo? If he ever wants something printed that they will not print in Italy, he should send it to Holland. Here we print what sells.
    globe,globes,map: Look at this globe: the whole world, with the coasts the Company's ships have found, New Holland and all. The Spanish keep their charts secret. I sell mine.
    tulips: Do not buy tulips. A man was offered three thousand guilders for one bulb of Semper Augustus, and would not sell. It cannot last.
  end
end
