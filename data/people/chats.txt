# Conversations people have with each other, which you may overhear now and then.
#
#   chat ID
#     between A B      who may have it: role or person ids (lists with commas), or anyone
#     where TEXT       only where the name of the place contains TEXT (optional)
#     if CONDITION     on A (with "player KEY" for what you know), as in roles.txt
#     ifb CONDITION    on B
#     again MINUTES    may be overheard again this long after; otherwise only once.
#     at once          a scene: begins as soon as the two stand together near you, before any other
#                      conversation and without waiting its turn; while you talk with someone else it waits
#     interrupt TEXT   if you are talking with one of the two, your conversation ends, and the game says TEXT (once
#                      its latest line has had time to be read: 4 seconds and one for every 25 letters)
#     within N         the two may stand up to N tiles apart (else 2): in a scene one may walk in as the other speaks
#                      (in a scene the two keep to their day: a line's effects can send one to the other)
#                      Give plot points an "again", so that they come round until you act.
#     A: TEXT {EFFECTS}
#     B: TEXT {EFFECTS}
#   end
#
# Effects (as in roles.txt) apply to the speaker; "hearer ..." to the other one;
# "player ..." and "name" (you learn the speaker's name; "hearer name": the other's)
# only if you are within earshot. In the text, $name $first $last are the speaker's,
# $other $otherfirst $otherlast the other's. *word* is highlighted: something to ask about.
# Everything you overhear is kept, to read again (key J).

earshot 8       # tiles
gap 180         # game minutes, at least, between two conversations you overhear
pairgap 1440    # game minutes before the same two people talk together again
chance 0.05     # each second, while two people stand together near you

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the plot

chat dialogue-rumour
  between martin student,scholar
  if not seek-dialogue
  again 1440
  B: Master $otherlast! Is it true, what they are saying at the Sorbonne, about a book from Italy? {hearer name}
  A: Quite true. Galileo of Florence has printed a *Dialogue* on the two chief systems of the world, Ptolemy's and *Copernicus*'s. And Copernicus has the better of it.
  B: Against Aristotle! Has anyone in Paris a copy?
  A: Not one. Father *Mersenne* would give his sandals for it. {player set heard-dialogue} {hearer set knows-dialogue}
end

chat roman-strings
  between antonio anyone
  if not player helped-lydia
  again 1440
  B: Good day, Signor $otherlast. What is new from Italy? {hearer name}
  A: Gut *strings* from Rome, the finest in Christendom, and not one musician in Paris has come to ask for them!
  B: There is a lady by the *Place Royale* who plays the lute; she has been lamenting a broken string all week. {player set heard-strings}
end

  if knows-dialogue
  ifb not knows-dialogue
  again 720
  A: Have you heard? An Italian has written a book saying that the Earth goes round the Sun!
  B: Then why do I not fall off? {set knows-dialogue}
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- London
# the Globe (people/london.txt, the ass's head): once you have met Robin (and read his greeting), Robin hears someone,
# and Kit Ashby comes in from the stage and takes the head for Nick Weaver's; Robin then gives you the sonnet (his line
# "hello if got-sonnet")
chat kit-sonnet
  between kit robin
  if player head-stuck and player robin-met and not kit-came
  at once
  within 12
  interrupt (The door from the stage opens.)
  B: (The head turns towards the stage door.) Hold, someone approaches. {hearer set kit-coming}
  A: (A young man comes in from the stage, still in Titania's gown, the paint half off his face. He does not see you in the shadow by the costumes.) Nick? You are still in it.
  A: (He laughs, and then does not.) No; leave it on. It is easier to say to the head than to you.
  A: Every afternoon at two I say "I love thee" to that face, and the yard laughs, and I do not mind, because I mean it, and nobody knows.
  A: This is my last season; next year my voice will be gone and I will be playing messengers. So I wrote it down.
  A: (He pushes a folded paper into the donkey's hand.) Don't answer. Read it after. (He runs out.) {set kit-came} {hearer set got-sonnet}
end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Rome

chat pasquino-today
  between anyone anyone
  if town=Rome
  A: Have you seen what was stuck on *Pasquino* this morning?
  B: Not before the police took it down. What did it say?
  A: That the Barberini bees have eaten all the honey in Rome, and now they are starting on the wax.
end

chat scheiner-in-the-shop
  between jesuit jesuit,priest,scholar
  if town=Rome
  again 1440
  A: Father *Scheiner* went into the bookshop by the Minerva yesterday, and there was the Florentine's Dialogue on the counter.
  B: And?
  A: He went white and then red, and offered ten scudi for it on the spot. He has been reading it all night, and making a list. {player set heard-scheiner}
end

chat the-protest
  between cardinal cardinal
  if town=Rome
  A: Did you see His Holiness's face when Borgia stood up in consistory?
  B: Everyone saw it. To be lectured on heresy by the King of Spain's man, before the whole College! There will be a reckoning.
  A: There already is. The Spanish cardinals have been told to go home to their dioceses.
end

chat the-sack
  between swissguard swissguard
  A: My grandfather's father was here in '27, when the Emperor's men sacked the city. A hundred and forty-seven of the Guard died on the steps of St Peter's.
  B: And the forty-two who lived brought the Pope out to Castel Sant'Angelo by the passage. We could do it again, if we had to.
end

chat the-catacombs
  between pilgrim pilgrim,friar,monk
  if town=Rome
  A: Will you come out to the *catacombs* with me tomorrow? On the Appian Way, past the Porta San Sebastiano.

end

  between student,scholar anyone
  where Pisa
  if player recent dropped-balls
  again 1440
