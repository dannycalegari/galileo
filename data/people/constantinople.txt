# The people of Constantinople.
# June 1632: Sultan Murad IV, twenty years old, has just taken the government into his own hands
# after the soldiers' revolt of May, and is said to walk the streets at night in disguise. The
# Patriarch Cyril Lucaris, a friend of the Protestant ambassadors, is hated by the Jesuits; the
# French ambassador Marcheville quarrels with everyone; Evliya, the son of the palace goldsmith,
# is twenty-one and dreams of travelling; Katip, a clerk of the army, of maps.
town Constantinople
names ottoman
prison Yedikule | The janissaries march you through the city to Yedikule, the Castle of the Seven Towers, where the Sultan keeps the ambassadors of the countries he is at war with. Your cell has been an ambassador's: there are Latin verses scratched on the wall, and a Venetian's calendar of four years. In the morning a clerk cannot find your name in any book, and lets you go.

    forgery,forged,forgeries,false,letters if not player gave-lucaris-letter: Forgeries are a subject I know too well, and do not discuss with strangers.
    name: Cyril, by the mercy of God Archbishop of Constantinople, New Rome, and Ecumenical Patriarch. {name}
    job: I keep the Orthodox faith alive under the Sultan, and I try to keep it alive under the Jesuits, which is harder.
    jesuits,enemies: The Jesuits of Galata hate me, because I read Calvin and correspond with the Dutch. They had my printing press broken up by the janissaries four years ago: they told the Grand Vizier I was printing sedition. I was printing the catechism.
    press,printing,printer: A Greek press, brought from London by a monk of ours. The janissaries smashed it in an afternoon. Ask the Dutch if they could send me another; their Elzevirs at Leiden print in every tongue.
    codex,bible,london,king: I sent the oldest Bible in the world to the King of England, four years ago: a book written in Alexandria twelve hundred years ago, in capitals, on vellum. Better in London than burned here.
    letter,haga,dutch if not player carried-lucaris-letter: Would you carry a letter for me, to the Dutch ambassador at Pera, Cornelis Haga? It should not go by my own people: some of them are paid by the Jesuits. {player add has:lucaris-letter 1} {player set carried-lucaris-letter}
    letter,haga,dutch: Into Haga's own hands, and nobody else's.
