# The people of Danzig.
# June 1632: Danzig, Lutheran and German-speaking, under the Polish Crown, is the granary of
# Europe: Polish rye floats down the Vistula and the Dutch carry it away. By the Truce of Altmark
# (1629) Sweden takes a share of the tolls of the Prussian ports. Peter Cruger teaches mathematics
# at the Academic Gymnasium; his best pupil, the brewer's son Johannes Hewelcke, is abroad.

names german
prison Stockturm | They lock you in the Stockturm, the prison tower by the Long Lane gate, under the torture chamber. All night you hear the gulls and the bells of St Mary's. In the morning they let you go.

person crane
  name Hans Kolbe
  title the crane master
  role labourer
  near Crane
  work landmark Crane
  talk
    name: Hans Kolbe, master of the town's Crane. {name}
    job: Inside the Crane are two great wheels, and men walk inside them like squirrels, and the wheels turn the drum, and the drum lifts four tons: masts into ships, wine casks out of them. The Crane is also a gate, and the town's best lookout. I am its master. I do not walk in the wheels any more.
  end
end

    job: I bring salt from Portugal and wine from France and herring from home, and I take away rye, as much as she will float with. The Hollanders call it the mother trade: without the Polish rye, Amsterdam would starve in a month.
    sound,toll,denmark,swedes: At the Sound the King of Denmark takes his toll, by the width of the deck, and since the truce the King of Sweden takes his toll here too. A man pays twice for being a Dutchman.
    ship,passage,sail: The Hope is in the river, loading. If you want passage, buy a ship of your own: I am a merchant, not a ferryman. The Swede below the Crane carries passengers, for Stockholm.

    job: I carry the King of Sweden's letters and his money between Danzig and Stockholm, and anyone who can pay: two livres, and as much again for a horse. Three days, if the wind serves.     job: My family came from Holland, Mennonites, and we make the Goldwasser: brandy with anise and herbs and flakes of real gold in it. Drink it and you swallow gold. The physicians say it is good for the heart. It is certainly good for mine.
