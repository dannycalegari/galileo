    book: My book on the motion of the heart, printed at Frankfurt four years ago. The physicians of Paris say I am mad. The physicians of Padua say I am right, which is worse for them.
    name: Doctor William Harvey, physician to the King, and to Saint Bartholomew's Hospital. {name}
    job: I am a physician. I have shown that the *blood* goes round the body.
    blood,circulation: It goes round, sir. Around and around, as the Earth goes round the Sun, if your Italian is right.

  end
