# Professions: what people of each trade wear, where they work, their day, and what
# they say. A person in a town file names a role and adds to it.
#
#   role ID "how they are called before you know their name"
#   sex m | f | x        (x: either; women then wear a dress and a coif)
#   look coat #hex legs #hex hat KIND #hex apron #hex dress robe halberd lute
#        (hat kinds: none hat cap coif helmet squarecap hood biretta)
#        old, beard tuft|full|moustache, stout: who they are (age, a beard, a paunch). Anyone whose look gives
#        none of these, and not "plain", has them drawn at random, and a height within 4% either way
#   work KIND            where they work: forge shop bakery inn church altar college
#                        theatre fortress gate market square quay field woods road mine
#                        landmark NAME (a building whose name contains NAME), none
#   sign EMBLEM          the sign hung over the door of their workshop (forge, shop, bakery):
#                        anvil pretzel scales book goblet palette (inns get a tankard and a lantern)
#   says TEXT            muttered now and then; "says A | B | C": one of them at random each time
#   approach [within N] if COND   (a person) when COND holds and you are near (within N steps; 30), they walk up to
#                        you and the conversation opens
#                        with their greeting; once (they remember "approached", the time: "hello if recent:0.01
#                        approached"); it waits while you talk, read, or watch a scene (People::approachStep)
#   sells ITEM N ...     a shopkeeper: what they sell (items.txt) and how many a day; you
#                        say "buy" (what they have, and the prices), "buy bread", "sell
#                        horseshoe" (they buy what they sell, at half price). A person's own
#                        "sells" replaces the role's.
#   money DENIERS        what they have to start with (12 deniers to the sou, 20 sous to the livre)
#   watch                of the watch: when someone cries "Thief!" the nearest of the watch in
#                        that town runs after you (main.cc, Game::theft); caught, you give the
#                        thing back and pay a fine (twice its price, five sous at least), or
#                        spend the night in the town's prison (a town file's "prison" line)
#   schedule             lines "HH:MM [sharp] ACTIVITY [PLACE] [if CONDITION]"; activities:
#                        sleep home eat work wander patrol go PLACE travel TOWN
#                        (go home-of ID: in that person's house, as one hidden there; go beside ID: next to
#                        that person, where they stand)
#                        Each person is up to 25 minutes early or late each day;
#                        "sharp" means on the minute, for everyone (a crowd on purpose)
#   talk ... end         lines "KEYWORDS [if CONDITION]: TEXT {EFFECTS}"
#   (in a town file, "crowd N ROLE" adds N people of a role; "crowd N ROLE work KIND ..."
#   gives them another place of work, as "crowd 8 swissguard work landmark Vatican")
#   (conversations between people, which you may overhear, are in chats.txt)
#   (in a town file, "guarded PLACE ... end" keeps a floor, such as Duke Humfrey's Library in the
#   Bodleian, for those with leave: "by ID" its guard, "pass if CONDITIONS" who may pass him,
#   "key ITEM" a key that opens it at any time, "stop TEXT {EFFECTS}" what he says when he turns
#   you back, "shut TEXT" what you find while he is away: then it is locked. Leaving is always
#   allowed. Code: main.cc, Game::mayEnter.)
#
# Gifts: "give strings", "give 2 sous". The answer is the line with the keyword
# give:ITEM (give:money for money, give:? for anything); the effect "take" means they keep
# it (it goes into their memory as has:ITEM). Without such a line they decline.
# Talk: the first line (own lines first, then the role's, then everyone's) whose
# keyword matches and whose condition holds is the answer. Keywords match on their
# first four letters, as in Ultima ("=word": that word only; "==words": only when it is all you
# say, "==good" but not "a good inn"). Special keywords: hello (when you begin), bye,
# and ? (anything else). *word* is shown highlighted. $name $first $role $town are
# filled in.
# Conditions: words joined by "and": KEY, not KEY, KEY >= 3, player KEY, met, asleep,
#   morning afternoon evening night, recent KEY (KEY holds a time, from "set KEY=now",
#   within the last three days), recent:N KEY (within the last N days). Every person remembers by themselves: met,
#   talks (how often you have spoken), last (when), asked:TOPIC. What is whose (main.cc):
#   whoever sees you carry off a thing remembers saw-thief and knows-thief (times); a
#   thing taken unseen leaves its keepers with robbed (its name) and robbed-at; you
#   remember thief:TOWN, wanted:TOWN (a day), thefts, arrests. "pay" (or "give ITEM")
#   to its keeper settles a thing taken in front of them; shopkeepers will not trade
#   with a recent thief.
# Effects, in braces: set KEY, set KEY=VALUE, unset KEY, add KEY N, learn SKILL,
#   player set KEY (and so on, for what you remember), name (you now know their
#   name), travel TOWN, bye, play PIECE (set KEY=now stores the time; $[KEY] in a text is
#   what they remember under KEY) (they play a piece of data/music.txt, and
#   stay where they are until it ends; it is heard from where they stand).
