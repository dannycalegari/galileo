# Contratacion; the silver fleet comes in every autumn. Zurbaran paints for the monasteries;
# Francisco Pacheco, Velazquez's master and father-in-law, censors paintings for the Holy Office;
# Bartolome Murillo is a boy of fourteen, an orphan apprenticed to a painter.

  near Casa de Contratacion
  work landmark Lonja
  money 24000
  talk
    name: Rodrigo de Vargas, cargador a Indias, of the consulado of Seville. {name}
    job: I send cloth, wine, oil and iron to New Spain and Peru on the fleets, and I am paid in silver, cochineal, sugar and tobacco when the fleets come back, if they come back. The Dutch took the whole silver fleet at Matanzas four years ago. I was not paid that year.
    fleet,silver,indies: The fleets sail in spring and come home in autumn, two of them, with the galleons to guard them: forty ships, a hundred, all of it through the Casa de Contratacion, which counts every real. This year the fleet is late.
    cochineal,grana if player heard-scarlet and not player scarlet-done: For a dyer in Rome? Then take the grana fina, the tame cochineal of Oaxaca, not the wild; they will know the difference in Rome by smell. I sell it by the pouch, cheaper than anyone in Italy will believe.
    tobacco,sugar,cochineal: Tobacco from Havana, sugar from the islands, cochineal from Oaxaca for the scarlet of every cardinal in Rome. Cheap here, dear everywhere else: that is the whole secret of Seville.
  end
end
