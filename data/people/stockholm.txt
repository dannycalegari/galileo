# The people of Stockholm.
# June 1632: the King is in Germany, at Nuremberg, and the Queen, Maria Eleonora, has gone to join
# him; their daughter Christina, five and a half, is at Tre Kronor in the care of the King's
# half-sister, Princess Catherine, Countess Palatine; the council governs at home; Louis De Geer,
# of Liege and Amsterdam, casts the King's guns and sells his copper. Every parish gives one man
# in ten, and the recruits sail from here for Stralsund to fill the King's regiments.
town Stockholm
names swedish
prison Tre Kronor | They lock you in a cellar of Tre Kronor, under the castle, where the water of the Norrstrom comes through the stones. In the morning they let you go, with a warning in Swedish that you do not understand.

    job: I keep the King's daughter while her mother is in Germany with him, and my own children with her, and the castle, and my temper.
    christina,princess,daughter: She is five, and she will be Queen. Her father has her taught as if she were a prince: Latin, history, riding, and the Bible. She learns faster than my son did, and he is nine. She asks every day for news of her father.
    queen,mother,maria,eleonora: The Queen has gone to Germany to be near the King. She did not take the child. She cried when she left; the child did not.
    king,brother,gustavus: My brother writes when he can. He is at Nuremberg, behind his ramparts, and the Emperor's general sits on a hill in front of him. That is all he tells me; the rest I read in the broadsheets from Hamburg.
  end
end

  name Gabriel Gustafsson Oxenstierna
  title of the King's council
  role scholar
  look coat #1E1E22 legs #1E1E22 hat hat #1A1A1E beard tuft
  near Tre Kronor
  work landmark Tre Kronor, the King's castle
  talk
    name: Gabriel Gustafsson Oxenstierna, of the council of the realm; my brother is the Chancellor, with the King in Germany. {name}
    job: While the King fights, the council finds him the money and the men. The copper of Falun pays for the regiments, and the parishes give the men, one in ten. Some parishes have no men left to give.
    war,cost,money,copper: Copper, iron, tar, and the tolls of the Prussian ports: that is how a kingdom of a million peasants pays for an army in the middle of Germany. And the French pay a subsidy, for the pleasure of seeing the Emperor beaten by Lutherans.
    recruits,conscription,soldiers,men: One man in ten, chosen by the parish. They sail from here to Stralsund, and walk from there to wherever the King is. Most of them do not come back; the sickness takes more than the Croats.
  end
end

    name: Louis De Geer, of Liege, of Amsterdam, and now, God help me, of Stockholm. {name}
    job: I cast the King's guns at my foundries in Ostergotland, and I sell his copper in Amsterdam, and I lend him money against the copper, and he pays me with the copper I have already sold. It is a circle, and I am in the middle of it.
    guns,cannon,foundry,iron: Cast iron guns, light enough for a regiment to drag along, and cheap enough to lose. The King's little guns won him Breitenfeld. I brought Walloon smiths from Liege to make them; the Swedes thought they were sorcerers.
    give:degeer-packet: (He breaks the seal on the quay, reads, and laughs.) My factor at Paris has sold the King's copper to the French, and the French have paid for it with the King's own subsidy. It is a circle. (He counts coins into your hand.) Six livres, as he promised you. And if you are going on to the King in Germany, the princess at the castle sends him a letter by every ship. {take} {player add money 1440} {player set degeer-delivered} {player set knows:stockholm:christina}
    copper,falun: The great mine at Falun: a hole in the ground so large you can lose a church in it. Copper is Sweden's silver. Buy it here; sell it in Amsterdam, if you can carry it.
  end
end

person ferryman
  name Olof Persson
  title a waterman of the Skeppsbron
  role waterman
  near Storkyrkan
  talk
    name: Olof Persson, waterman, of the Skeppsbron, these thirty years. {name}
    job: I row people across to Sodermalm and out to the ships, and back, and I know every rock in this harbour. For Danzig, there is the King's post ship at the quay.
    vasa,ship,wreck,sank: The Vasa. (He points out across the water.) There, off Beckholmen, not a mile from the quay. Four years ago, in August, on her first voyage, with all her flags flying and the whole town watching. A puff of wind, and she lay over, and the water came in at the gunports, and she went down like a stone with thirty men. Too tall, too narrow, and too many guns; the King wanted two decks of them. Nobody says so aloud.
    malaren,lake,saltsjon,sea: West of the town is the lake, sweet water, a hundred miles of it; east is the Saltsjon, salt, and the sea. The town sits in the gap, and the water runs through it under the bridges, east, always east.
  end
end
