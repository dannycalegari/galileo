# The people of Vienna.
# June 1632: the Emperor Ferdinand II holds his court at the Hofburg, with his Jesuit confessor,
# William Lamormaini, at his elbow; the Empress Eleonora of Mantua has brought Italian music with
# her; Sebastian Tengnagel keeps the Imperial Library and its Turkish and Arabic manuscripts.
town Vienna
names german
prison Schranne | They lock you in the cellars of the Schranne, the town court on the Hoher Markt. A clerk asks your name, your religion, and whether you have read any Lutheran books. In the morning they let you go.

  look old
  near University of Vienna
  work landmark University of Vienna
  talk
    name: William Lamormaini, of the Society of Jesus, from Luxembourg: rector of the college, and confessor to His Majesty. {name}
    job: I hear the Emperor's confession every week, and every day I remind him that a Catholic prince must give back to God what the heretics took. He listens. He is a very good man.
    edict,restitution,church: Three years ago His Majesty commanded the Protestants to give back every church and abbey they had taken since the Peace of Passau. The Protestants answered by inviting the King of Sweden. God tries those He loves.
    sweden,gustavus,king: The King of Sweden is a scourge, sent for our sins. Scourges break. I do not say when.
  end

person valentini
  name Giovanni Valentini
  title the Emperor's Kapellmeister
  role musician
  look coat #3A1E2A legs #2A1E22 hat hat #1E1A1E beard tuft
  near Hofburg
  work landmark Hofburg
  talk
    name: Giovanni Valentini, of Venice, Kapellmeister to His Imperial Majesty. {name}
    job: I direct the Emperor's chapel: forty singers and players, Italians most of them, for the masses, the vespers and the Empress's entertainments. I write masses for twenty voices, and sonatas for anything that has strings.
    empress,eleonora,mantua,opera: Her Majesty the Empress Eleonora is a Gonzaga of Mantua, where they have had music dramas since Monteverdi's Orfeo. She brought Mantua to Vienna with her. The Viennese are learning to sit still for three hours. Slowly.
    monteverdi,venice: Signor Monteverdi is master of music at Saint Mark's, and a priest now, since the plague took so many. He is the greatest of us. He writes for the voice as if it were the soul.
  end
end

person tengnagel
  name Sebastian Tengnagel
  title the Imperial Librarian
  role scholar
  look beard tuft
  near Hofburg
  talk
    give:dialogue if has:dialogue >= 1: The Library has one already. One is all a library needs; that is the whole idea of a library.
    give:dialogue: (He writes the title in a great book.) The Imperial Library thanks you. Whatever they do with it in Rome, here it will be kept, and catalogued, and dusted, until the end of the world. I am a Catholic, and a librarian: we keep books. Burning them is for amateurs. {take}
    name: Sebastian Tengnagel, of Gelderland, Imperial Librarian. {name}
    job: I keep the Emperor's library, and I buy for it every Turkish, Arabic and Persian book I can find. I read them too. It is the only way to know what the Turks think of us.
    dioscurides,herbal,busbecq,manuscript: Old Busbecq, the Emperor Ferdinand's ambassador to the Sultan, found a Dioscurides in Constantinople, written for a princess eleven hundred years ago, with every plant painted, and the Emperor Maximilian bought it. It is the most beautiful book in the world, and it is mine. That is, it is the Emperor's. It lies open in the Hofburg; go and look.
    arabic,turkish,persian if player skill:arabic: You read Arabic? Then you are the first visitor this year with whom I can talk properly. Sit. No, sit: I have a Persian poem that will take all afternoon.
    arabic,turkish,persian: I taught myself Arabic and Turkish from grammars and from prisoners of war. The prisoners were better.
  end
end
