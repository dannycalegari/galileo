# rumour that sends the King of Sweden up the hill (events.txt alte-veste).

  talk
    name: Johann Lederer, pastor of Zirndorf, of the Lutheran faith, under the Margrave of Ansbach. {name}
    job: I baptise them, I marry them, and this summer I bury them. Half my parish has run to Nuremberg, behind the walls; the other half is hiding in the woods with the cows.
    alte,veste,castle,ruin: The Alte Veste? An old castle of the burgraves on the ridge, burned three hundred years ago. The children play in it. There is nothing up there but nettles and a good view of Nuremberg, which is why every army that comes wants it.
    wallenstein,duke,army,camp if player event:wallenstein-comes: They came up the road from the east with their banners and their carts, more of them than the whole of Franconia, and cut down my woods for their huts in a single day. They took my church bell for their guns. God forgive them; I cannot.
    wallenstein,duke,army,camp: Everyone says the Duke of Friedland is coming from Bohemia, with the whole Imperial army. Everyone says he will sit down on a hill and wait. I pray it is not our hill.
  end
end

  name Albrecht von Wallenstein
  title the Duke of Friedland, the Emperor's general
  role prince
  look coat #1A1A1E legs #1A1A1E hat hat #8A1A1A
  near farm by the Alte Veste
  work landmark farm by the Alte Veste

    0:00 work if player event:wallenstein-comes and player not event:siege-ends
  talk
    # the Duke's offer, once you have seen his guns in the ruin and before you go to Monro: thirty
    # livres to carry the astrologer's lie, sworn; paid after the storming (nuremberg.txt monro; ending.txt "the other side")
    hello if player sold-north and player event:assault-over and not player paid-by-duke: (He counts it out himself, thirty livres in gold, and pushes it across the table without a word.) The King of Sweden came up my hill. Go quietly. {player add money 7200} {player set paid-by-duke}
    hello if player saw-north-guns and not player sold-north and not player refused-duke and not player storming-set and not player event:siege-ends: (He does not look up from his foot.) You have been in my ruin. (Now he looks at you, as if you were a horse he had decided to buy.) Good: then you know what the King of Sweden does not. Go back to your Scot and tell him what my astrologer told you, and swear that you saw it yourself; and when the King has come up my hill, there will be thirty livres for you here. Well? {set offered-sale=now}
    yes,=i will,deal,agreed,swear,take if recent:0.05 offered-sale: (He nods, once, and looks back at his foot.) Go quietly. {unset offered-sale} {player set sold-north}
    no,=never,refuse,=i will not if recent:0.05 offered-sale: (He shrugs.) Then tell him what you like. He will come up my hill all the same; they always do. Go quietly, before I change my mind about letting you go at all. {unset offered-sale} {player set refused-duke}
    hello: (He sits with one foot wrapped in flannel on a stool, and looks at you as if you were a horse he might buy.) Speak quietly. I have forbidden every dog in the camp to bark, and every cock to crow.
    name: Albrecht von Wallenstein, Duke of Friedland and of Mecklenburg, general of the Emperor's armies, for the second time. {name}
    job: I raise armies, and I pay them, and I do not lose them. The King of Sweden wants a battle. I shall not give him one. I shall sit on this hill until his men have eaten their horses, and then their boots.
    gout,foot: The gout. The doctors bleed me, the astrologer tells me it will pass when Saturn leaves the house of health. Saturn is taking his time.
    stars,horoscope,kepler,astrology,astrologer: Kepler cast my nativity, twenty-four years ago, without knowing whose it was: restless, contemptuous of law, greedy of power, many enemies, and lucky. He was right in every word. He died two years ago in Regensburg, poor; I owed him money. Senno does my stars now. He is a cheerful man, which in an astrologer is a fault.
    king,sweden,gustavus,swedes: A great soldier and a poor gambler. He will attack me, because he cannot bear to wait. They never can.
    nuremberg,siege: Nuremberg has fifty thousand mouths inside its walls and its lines, and nothing coming in but what my Croats let through. Which is nothing.
    senno,report,rumour,north,ruin,guns if player warned-monro and player event:assault-over: (He looks at you as at a horse that has kicked him.) You went into my ruin, and then to your Scot. It made no difference: the King came up my hill all the same. They always do.
    senno,report,rumour,north if player event:assault-over: (He almost smiles.) My astrologer tells me you were most helpful. I do not ask how. Go back to the King of Sweden and tell him the Duke of Friedland sends his compliments, and has kept his guns.
    bye: Go quietly.
  end
end

  talk
    # once you have told Monro of Wallenstein, the astrologer is the Duke's bait: his greeting is the lie
    hello if player reported-wallenstein and not player heard-weak-north and not player storming-set and not player event:siege-ends: (A round, cheerful man in a dark gown, with a glass in his hand, who seems very pleased to see you.) A stranger! Come, sit: the Duke's wine is better than his temper. (He fills a cup for you, and leans close.) Shall I tell you what the stars say? That the King of Sweden will come up this hill before the month is out. And shall I tell you what the stars do not say, but I do? The Duke has drawn his guns off the north end of the ridge, by the old castle, to the south side, where he thinks the attack will come. There is nothing by the ruin but a few Croats and the nettles. (He puts a finger to his lips.) You did not hear it from me. {player set heard-weak-north}
    name: Giovanni Battista Senno, of Genoa, mathematician and astrologer to the Duke of Friedland. {name}
    job: I read the heavens for the Duke, which is the safest place to read them: nobody can say they have been misquoted.
    stars,horoscope,astrology,heavens if not player heard-weak-north: (He lowers his voice, and his breath smells of the Duke's wine.) Shall I tell you what the stars say? That the King of Sweden will come up this hill before the month is out. And shall I tell you what the stars do not say, but I do? The Duke has drawn his guns off the north end of the ridge, by the old castle, to the south side, where he thinks the attack will come. There is nothing by the ruin but a few Croats and the nettles. You did not hear it from me. {player set heard-weak-north}
    stars,horoscope,astrology,heavens,north,guns,weak if player event:assault-over and player gave-false-report: (He beams.) The stars were right, were they not? The King came up the hill. And the Duke was very pleased with somebody. With you, perhaps; with me, certainly.
    stars,horoscope,astrology,heavens,north,guns,weak: I have told you too much already. It is the wine.
    galileo,padua: I studied at Padua, where they still talk of Galileo's lectures on the new star of 1604: the heavens can change, he said. The Duke pays me to say what they will change into.
  end
end
