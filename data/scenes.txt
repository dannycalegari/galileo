# The still scenes at the start of a new game, after you choose who you are.
# Each is a view of the world held still, with a few lines; a click (or Space) goes on, Esc skips
# them all (main.cc, Game::startScenes).
#
#   scene ... end
#     id NAME                        its name (stable: never reuse one); a picture data/art/scenes/NAME.png, made by
#                                    galileo --still NAME, is shown in place of the world (main.cc Game::stillFor)
#     in TOWN                        positions are in this town (or hamlet)
#     time HH:MM                     the light of that hour
#     look PLACE yaw D pitch D zoom Z [roofs]   what the camera looks at, and from where
#     caption TEXT                   the words (*word*: lit, as in people's lines)
#     you PLACE [child|youth] [facing F] [kneeling] [hat]     you, as you chose to be
#     cow PLACE [facing F] [lying]   Pâquerette (animals.txt "paquerette")
#     coins PLACE                    the week's milk money, in piles on a table
#     ID PLACE [facing F] [carries baby|pails|cap|book] [bareheaded]   a person (town:id, _ for a space)
#                                    (any figure may also be "kneeling", or "seated" on a chair)
#                                    (or "robe #hex", "gown #hex": a woman's gown; "habit": a friar's,
#                                    the hood up; "asshead": the stage's ass's head)
#   PLACE: COL,ROW of the town's map (row 0 at the top), or door:NAME, outside:NAME, table:NAME,
#   hearth:NAME, bed:NAME, well:NAME, bench:NAME (in that building), then +DCOL,DROW
#   F: N NE E SE S SW W NW, cam (towards the camera), or to:WHO (you, cow, coins or an ID)
#   (a baby is not a figure of its own: its mother "carries baby", with your skin)
#
#   arrive TOWN PLACE facing F      where you stand when the scenes are over
