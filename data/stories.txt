story the-end
  title The end of the story
  main
  top
  step if player ending-seen: Your part in Galileo's story is over. The roads are still open, and you may go on travelling and trading as you please.
  step well if player ending-seen and player man-history: Galileo abjured, and was not burned, nor kept in a cell: he lived out his life at Arcetri.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player man-worse: Galileo was kept under guard in Rome, and never went home.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player man-worst: Galileo was condemned on a forged paper, and died in a cell of the Holy Office.
  step well if player ending-seen and player book-history: The Dialogue was not burned: put into Latin, it went all over Europe.
  step well if player ending-seen and player book-latin: The Dialogue was put into Latin, and went all over Europe; but few copies of the Italian were kept.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player book-kept: The copies of the Dialogue were kept, but there was no Latin translation.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player book-worse: Some copies of the Dialogue survived, read by a few; there was no Latin translation.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player book-worst: The seizures succeeded, and the Dialogue was hardly seen again.
  step well if player ending-seen and player science-printed: The Two New Sciences was printed at Leiden, in 1638.
  step badly if player ending-seen and player science-seized: The Two New Sciences was seized on the road and burned.
end
