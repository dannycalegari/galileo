# Architecture styles.  Each town names one; landmarks may name another.
#   walls    colours of plaster or stone to choose from
#   trim     window sills, string courses
#   roofs    roof colours to choose from
#   roof     gable, hip, mansard, flat: one or more, chosen house by house
#   storeys  fewest and most storeys, counting the ground floor
#   storey   height of an upper storey (a tile is 1)
#   pitch    roof height divided by half the house's width
#   timber   chance (0 to 1, or yes/no) of half-timbered upper storeys, jettied out over the street
#   dormers  yes: dormer windows (mansard roofs only)
#   gablefront yes: the gable end faces the street
#   jetty    yes: each half-timbered storey juts out over the one below
#   balustrade yes: a balustrade around flat roofs
#   chimney  colour, and how far chimneys rise above the ridge
