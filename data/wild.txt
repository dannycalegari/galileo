# The open country (main.cc, Game::wildStep): brigands on the roads, wolves and bears, deer to
# hunt. Every 25 tiles you travel outside the towns and post houses, the game weighs what you
# might meet. The chances are per hundred tiles travelled; a meeting needs you some way from any
# town (brigands 30 tiles, wolves 25, bears 40, deer 15), and there is a pause after each (four
# hours for brigands, three for wolves, ten for a bear).
#
#   brigands DAY NIGHT       a band of two to four on the road (half as often off the roads; more
#                            in woods and hills)
#   wolves NIGHT DUSK        a pack of two to four (five in winter), in woods or hills, at night,
#                            and at dusk and dawn from November to February (less on horseback)
#   bears CHANCE             one, in the hills or mountains
#   deer DAY DUSK            a herd of two to five, in woods, by day; more at dusk and dawn
#   escort F                 with a hired sword beside you, brigands come F as often (wolves half)
#   land NAME brigands F wolves F bears F
#                            the country's factor on each (the "country" of its nearest town's
#                            people file; France where none is given)
# In the prologue (a game begun as Mersenne's courier, until he has his Dialogue) there are no
# brigands, wolves or bears: its errands need the money you carry. Deer and fish, yes.
#
#   royal TOWN RADIUS OWNER | NAME
#                            the woods within RADIUS tiles of TOWN are OWNER's: a shot there may
#                            bring a gamekeeper, and a deer is poaching (ten livres, or prison)
